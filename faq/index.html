Emergencies

What should I do first when I find water damage?

Stop the water at the fixture valve or the main shutoff, then turn off power to wet areas if it is safe. Take photos and call a restoration company. See our full first 24 hours checklist.

Where is the main water shutoff in a Buckeye home?

In most homes it is on the front wall of the house, in the garage or near the water heater. There is also a shutoff in the meter box near the street.

Do you really answer at night and on weekends?

Yes. We answer day and night, every day of the year, including holidays.

Is it safe to stay in my home with water damage?

Usually, as long as the power is safe, the ceiling is not sagging and there is no sewage or floodwater in living areas. If any of those apply, keep people and pets away from the affected area.

What if I cannot find where the water is coming from?

Turn off the main shutoff and call us. Finding the source is part of every inspection.

Drying and damage

How long does drying take?

Most homes take about three to five days. Concrete slabs, hardwood and thick cabinets can take longer. We measure moisture until everything reads dry.

Can I just use fans to dry my home?

Fans dry surfaces, but they rarely dry what is inside walls, under tile or inside cabinets. Buckeye's dry air can make a floor feel dry while the slab underneath is still wet.

Will you have to tear out my walls or floors?

Only what cannot be dried or is contaminated. Our goal is to save as much as possible. We explain what we recommend and why before anything is removed.

Can wet carpet be saved?

Often, if it was clean water and drying starts quickly. Carpet soaked by floodwater or sewage usually needs to be replaced.

Why is water damage so common in Buckeye if it is so dry?

Hard water wears out water heaters and plumbing early, slab foundations hide leaks, AC systems run nonstop in summer and monsoon storms bring sudden heavy rain.

Mold

How fast can mold grow after water damage?

Mold can start growing within about 24 to 48 hours on wet materials, and faster in summer heat.

How do I know if I have hidden mold?

A musty smell, peeling paint, staining, warped cabinet bottoms or a history of leaks are common signs. Moisture readings and a look inside the wall confirm it.

Will mold come back after it is removed?

Not if the moisture source is fixed and the area is properly dried. Mold comes back when moisture comes back.

Cost and insurance

How much does water damage restoration cost in Buckeye?

Most jobs fall between about $2,000 and $7,500. Small leaks cost less. Large floods, sewage or mold can cost $15,000 or more. See our cost guide.

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. We inspect and give you a written estimate before any work begins.

Does homeowners insurance cover water damage?

Most policies cover sudden and accidental water damage, like burst pipes and appliance failures. Flood, sewer backup and slow leaks are often treated differently. See our insurance claim guide.

Can you work with my insurance company?

Yes. We document the damage with photos, moisture readings and a written scope, and we can work directly with your adjuster.

Does insurance cover monsoon flooding?

Outside water that rises and flows in usually needs flood insurance. Rain that comes through a storm damaged roof or window is often covered by homeowners insurance.

Our service

Do you do repairs or only water removal?

Both. We remove the water, dry the structure, clean and sanitize, and repair drywall, flooring, baseboards and more.

Do you serve businesses?

Yes. We help offices, shops, restaurants, warehouses, rental properties and HOA buildings.

What parts of Buckeye do you serve?

Every neighborhood in the city of Buckeye, in ZIP codes 85326 and 85396. See our service areas.

Do you fix the leak itself?

We handle the water damage. The source, like a pipe, roof or AC line, is repaired by a plumber, roofer or HVAC technician. We work alongside them so the timing is right.
