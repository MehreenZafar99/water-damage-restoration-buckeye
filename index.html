Water in your home right now? Do this first

  1. Stop the water. Turn off the main shutoff valve near the front of the house or in the garage.
  2. Stay safe around power. If water is near outlets or appliances, turn off power at the breaker only if you can reach it without standing in water.
  3. Move what matters. Lift rugs, electronics, papers and photos off wet floors.
  4. Take photos. Photograph the water, the source and damaged items for insurance.

Why water damage in Buckeye is different

Buckeye is dry most of the year, but water damage is still common. The causes here are just different.

Very hard water

Minerals wear out water heaters, valves and supply lines early. When a tank fails, it can dump 40 to 50 gallons across a garage or closet floor.

Leaks under the slab

Most homes sit on concrete. When a line under the slab leaks, water can spread under tile and flooring for weeks before anyone sees it.

Monsoon season

From July through September, heavy storms fill washes and push water under doors, through garages and past roof tiles.

Stucco and tile roofs

These surfaces look solid, but they can hide moisture. A small roof or window leak can soak framing long before a stain shows.

New homes still leak

Thousands of new plumbing connections means some fail in the first few years. Water damage in a new home is more common than most buyers expect.

AC systems run nonstop

Summer heat keeps condensate lines working hard. A clog can overflow into an attic, closet or ceiling below the air handler.

  1. InspectFind the source and map where water went with moisture meters and thermal cameras.

  5. Repair and restoreRepair or replace drywall, baseboards, flooring and cabinets.

We work with your insurance company

Frequently asked questions

How fast should I call after finding water damage?

Right away. Water keeps moving into drywall, subfloor and cabinets every hour it sits. Mold can start within 24 to 48 hours.

Can I just dry it myself with fans?

Fans help surfaces, but they rarely dry inside walls, under tile or beneath cabinets. Moisture meters are the only way to know it is dry.

Does homeowners insurance cover water damage in Arizona?

Most policies cover sudden water damage, like a burst pipe or appliance failure. Slow leaks and outside flooding are often treated differently.

Do you handle the repairs too, or just the drying?

Both. We remove the water, dry the structure, then repair drywall, flooring, baseboards and cabinets.

What causes most water damage in Buckeye?

Water heater failures, slab leaks, burst supply lines, AC drain overflows and monsoon storms. Hard water wears out tanks and fittings early.

Is water from a monsoon flood dangerous?

It can be. Outside floodwater picks up soil, waste and sewage, so it needs cleaning, not just drying.

Do you help businesses as well as homes?

Yes. We handle offices, shops, warehouses and rental properties across Buckeye.

Do I need to be home while the equipment runs?

Not the whole time. Drying usually runs a few days. We set it up, check readings on each visit, and remove equipment as areas dry.
