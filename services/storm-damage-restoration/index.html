What monsoon storms do to Buckeye homes

Buckeye's monsoon season runs roughly from mid June through September, with most of the big storms in July and August. These storms can build fast and bring a combination of:

  • Strong winds that can lift and crack roof tiles, break windows and knock over trees and fences
  • Dust storms that come ahead of the rain and coat everything in fine dirt
  • Heavy, sudden rain that overwhelms roofs, gutters and yard drainage
  • Flash flooding in washes, low streets and yards
  • Hail now and then, which can crack tiles and skylights

The damage usually comes in pairs. Wind opens up the house, and rain comes right in behind it.

Types of storm damage we handle

Roof leaks from wind damage

Blown off or cracked tiles let rain reach the underlayment and attic. See ceiling and roof leak damage.

Broken windows and doors

Rain blowing through a broken window soaks carpet, drywall and furniture fast.

Water pushed under doors

Wind driven rain and pooled water can come under patio doors and garage doors.

Flooding from outside water

When yards and streets flood, water can enter at ground level. See flood damage cleanup.

Fallen trees and debris

Trees and large branches can puncture roofs and walls, opening the home to rain.

Dust and mud inside

After a storm, the mix of dust and water can leave a muddy film on floors, walls and belongings.

What to do after a storm

  1. Stay safe. Watch for downed power lines, broken glass and weak ceilings.
  2. Photograph everything. Take pictures of the roof, windows, yard and every damaged room before cleaning up. Include a wide shot and close ups.
  3. Prevent more damage. Cover broken windows and move items away from leaks, but only if it is safe to do so.
  4. Keep receipts for anything you buy for emergency repairs, like tarps or plastic sheeting.
  5. Call your insurance company to start the claim.
  6. Call (623) 555-0148 for inspection and cleanup.

How we restore storm damage

  1. Emergency protection. Tarping openings and boarding broken windows to stop more water from getting in.
  2. Water removal. Extracting water from floors and carpet.
  3. Drying. Air movers and dehumidifiers for walls, floors, attics and framing.
  4. Cleanup. Removing mud, dust and debris and cleaning affected surfaces.
  5. Removing damaged materials that cannot be saved.
  6. Repairs. Drywall, insulation, ceilings, flooring and paint.

Structural repairs to the roof or exterior may need a roofer or contractor. We document the interior and exterior damage to help with repairs and your insurance claim.

Storm damage and insurance

Wind damage to your roof and the rain that comes in through that damage are often covered by homeowners insurance. Water that rises from the ground and flows in is usually a flood claim, which needs separate flood insurance. Clear photos taken right after the storm make a big difference in how smoothly a claim goes. See our insurance claim guide.

What storm damage restoration usually costs

Costs vary more with storm damage than with most jobs, because it depends on how the water got in and how much of the home it reached. We inspect, document and give you a written estimate before work starts.

Frequently asked questions

When is monsoon season in Buckeye?

Officially from mid June through September. Most of the strongest storms hit in July and August.

Should I wait for my insurance adjuster before cleaning up?

No. Take photos, then start preventing more damage right away. Most policies expect you to protect the home from further harm.

Can you tarp my roof?

We can provide emergency protection like tarping and boarding to keep water out until a roofer makes permanent repairs.

Is storm damage covered by insurance?

Wind damage and rain coming in through that damage are often covered. Rising floodwater usually needs flood insurance.

How do I know if my roof was damaged if I cannot see it?

Stains on ceilings, a wet attic or loose tiles in the yard are signs. We check the attic and ceilings during our inspection.

Do you handle storm damage for businesses?

Yes. We help shops, offices and warehouses dry out and reopen after storms.
