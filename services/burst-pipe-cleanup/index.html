First, shut off the water

If a pipe is spraying or pouring right now, turn off the main shutoff valve before anything else. In most Buckeye homes it is near the front of the house, by the garage, or next to the water heater. If you cannot find it, the city meter box near the street also has a shutoff.

Why pipes burst in Buckeye

People think of burst pipes as a cold weather problem. In Buckeye, freezing is only one cause, and not the biggest one.

High water pressure

Some Buckeye neighborhoods have high incoming water pressure. Over years, that pressure stresses fittings, valves and flexible supply lines until one gives out. A failing pressure regulator can make it worse.

Hard water wear

Buckeye's very hard water leaves mineral buildup inside valves and fittings. Over time, that scale causes weak spots and stuck parts that can crack.

Failed supply lines

The braided lines under sinks, behind toilets and on water heaters are one of the most common failure points. They are cheap parts, but when one bursts it releases water at full pressure.

Cold snaps

Buckeye does get nights in the 30s during winter. Pipes in garages, exterior walls, attics and outdoor hose bibs can crack in a hard freeze, then leak once they thaw.

Construction and settling

Fittings that were not seated properly in newer homes, or pipes stressed by soil movement, can let go without warning.

Where burst pipe water goes

A burst pipe inside a wall is the worst kind. Water runs down inside the wall cavity, soaks insulation and the bottom plate, and spreads out under the baseboards on both sides. You might see water in the room next door before you see it where the pipe broke.

Pipes in the attic are another concern in Buckeye homes. When they break, water comes through ceiling drywall, often bringing insulation down with it.

How we handle burst pipe damage

  1. Confirm the water is off and the area is safe.
  2. Extract standing water from floors and carpet.
  3. Open what needs opening. Sometimes baseboards come off or a small section of drywall is removed to reach wet insulation and framing.
  4. Dry the structure with air movers and dehumidifiers, checking moisture daily.
  5. Repair drywall, baseboards, paint and flooring.

We focus on water damage. Once the water is off, you will also need the pipe itself fixed by a plumber. We can work alongside your plumber so repairs and drying happen in the right order.

Why speed matters

Clean water from a burst pipe is the easiest kind of water damage to fix, but only if drying starts quickly. After a day or two, clean water can turn into a mold problem, especially inside walls where air does not move. Starting early usually means less demolition and a lower bill.

What burst pipe cleanup usually costs

A small burst pipe caught quickly can be one of the lower cost water damage jobs. A pipe that ran for hours while you were at work can affect several rooms. Most jobs fall in the range of about $2,000 to $7,500. You get a written estimate after we inspect.

Insurance for burst pipes

A sudden burst pipe is usually covered by homeowners insurance. Many policies do not cover the pipe repair itself, but they do cover the water damage it caused. We document the damage for your claim.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my water shutoff valve?

In most Buckeye homes it is on the front wall of the house, in the garage or near the water heater. There is also a shutoff at the meter box near the street.

Do you fix the broken pipe?

We handle the water damage: removal, drying and repairs to the home. The pipe itself should be repaired by a plumber, and we can coordinate timing with them.

Will you have to cut my walls open?

Sometimes a small section, or just the baseboards. It depends on how much water got inside the wall. We only remove what is needed to dry properly.

Can pipes really freeze in Buckeye?

Yes, on the coldest winter nights. Pipes in garages, attics, exterior walls and outdoor spigots are the most at risk.

How long does drying take after a burst pipe?

Usually three to five days for typical drywall and framing. Thicker materials or wet slabs can take longer.

What if the pipe burst while I was away?

Call us as soon as you find it. We will check how far the water spread and whether mold has started. The sooner we start, the more we can save.
