Water damage help for Westpark homes

Westpark is an established Buckeye neighborhood with rows of tract homes built largely in the 2000s. Homes sit close together with similar layouts and similar plumbing. That also means many of them are going through the same stage of life at the same time: original parts are wearing out.

Common water damage risks in Westpark

Water heaters past their prime

Buckeye's hard water wears out tank water heaters early. Many Westpark homes have heaters in the garage. When a tank fails, water runs across the garage floor, under the door and into the house.

Original supply lines

The flexible lines on sinks, toilets and water heaters are designed to be replaced, but many never are. After many years of hard water and pressure, they can burst without warning.

Slab leaks

Westpark homes sit on slab foundations. Leaks in lines under the concrete can spread under tile and carpet for a long time before they show. Warm floor spots and damp grout are common signs.

Toilet fill valves and wax rings

Worn toilet parts cause slow leaks that soak the bathroom subfloor and nearby baseboards.

Close neighbors, shared concerns

Homes built close together can share drainage paths. During heavy monsoon rain, water from one yard can move into the next if grading is not right.

Why Westpark homeowners should check their water heater

Look at the label on your water heater for the manufacture date. If it is more than about 8 years old, or you see rust, crusty deposits or water around the base, it is worth having a plumber check it. Replacing a tank before it fails is far cheaper than cleaning up after one.

How we help

Frequently asked questions

Why are water heaters failing so often in Westpark?

Many are at or past the age when Buckeye's hard water wears them out. Garage tanks that fail can flood into the house.

Can garage water really get into my house?

Yes. It often runs under the interior door and soaks the bottom of the shared wall with the laundry room or kitchen.

I rent my home. Who should call you?

Either the tenant or the owner can call. We document the damage and can keep both informed.

How do I know if water got into my walls?

Swollen baseboards, bubbling paint and a musty smell are signs. We confirm with moisture meters.

Do you repair the damage after drying?

Yes. We repair drywall, baseboards and flooring once everything is dry.

Is a slow leak under a sink an emergency?

It can become one. Slow leaks soak cabinets and subfloor and can lead to mold. Get it checked soon.
