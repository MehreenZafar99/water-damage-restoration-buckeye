Water damage help for Riata West homes

Common water damage risks in Riata West

Water heaters

Buckeye's hard water shortens water heater life. In homes built around 2008 to 2010, many original tanks have been replaced, and some replacement tanks are now aging too. A failing tank can release a lot of water into a garage or closet.

Washing machine hoses

Rubber washer hoses crack with age. In homes of this age, original hoses are well past the point where they should have been replaced.

Toilet fill valves and supply lines

Worn fill valves can cause slow overflows, and aging supply lines can burst under pressure.

Dishwasher and sink leaks

Worn seals and loose connections under the kitchen sink can leak slowly into cabinets and subfloor for weeks before anyone notices.

AC systems reaching replacement age

Many AC systems in homes of this age are nearing the end of their life. Older units, rusted drain pans and clogged condensate lines can lead to overflows in attics and closets.

Roof underlayment

Tile roof underlayment in homes of this age can begin to wear from years of sun, letting monsoon rain through.

A maintenance checklist for Riata West homes

  • Check your water heater's age and look for rust or leaks at the base.
  • Replace rubber washer hoses with braided steel hoses.
  • Inspect the supply lines under every sink and toilet.
  • Look under the kitchen sink and around the dishwasher for swelling or stains.
  • Have the AC condensate line flushed each spring.
  • Have the roof inspected before monsoon season.

Frequently asked questions

Why are water heaters failing so often in Riata West?

Homes here are at the age when Buckeye's hard water has worn out original tanks and is wearing on replacements.

How often should washer hoses be replaced?

About every five years. Braided steel hoses are a safer choice than rubber.

How can I tell if my dishwasher is leaking?

Look for a swollen toe kick, soft flooring in front of the machine or stains under the sink.

Does insurance cover a failed water heater?

The water damage from a sudden failure is usually covered. The heater itself usually is not.

Can you work with my plumber?

Yes. We handle the water damage and can plan drying around the plumber's repair.
