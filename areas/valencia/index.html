Common water damage risks in Valencia

Older supply pipes

Older homes may still have original pipe materials. Galvanized steel supply lines, for example, rust from the inside and narrow with mineral buildup over the years. They leak at joints and can burst at weak spots.

Aging drain and sewer lines

Older drain lines and sewer laterals can crack, sag or be invaded by roots. A blockage can send sewage back up into the lowest drains in the home.

Roofs and flat sections

Older roofs, patio covers and flat sections may have worn coatings or underlayment. Monsoon rain can find its way through and into attics and ceilings.

Water heaters

Hard water shortens water heater life everywhere in Buckeye. In older homes, heaters in closets or garages may also sit on floors or platforms that have taken water before.

Past repairs

Older homes often have a history of patches and repairs. Some are solid, some are not. A past fix that was never done right can be where the next leak starts.

Care for older homes

Older homes deserve a careful approach. Before we remove anything, we check whether it can be dried in place. Original wood, plaster and trim can often be saved with controlled drying. When something truly cannot be saved, we explain why before it is removed.

Signs your older home needs a plumbing check

  • Low water pressure throughout the house
  • Rusty or discolored water, especially first thing in the morning
  • Frequent small leaks at different fixtures
  • Gurgling drains or more than one slow drain at once
  • Visible rust or corrosion on exposed pipes

If you notice these, a plumber can check your lines before they lead to a bigger water problem.

Frequently asked questions

Are older homes in Valencia more likely to have leaks?

Older pipes, drains and roofs do fail more often with age, especially after years of Buckeye's hard water and heat.

Should I replace old galvanized pipes?

If your home still has them and you see low pressure or rusty water, it is worth talking to a plumber about repiping.

Can old plaster walls be dried?

Often, yes, with careful drying. We try to save them before considering removal.

Why does my older home have sewer backups?

Older sewer lines can crack, sag or fill with roots. A plumber can camera the line to find out.

Does insurance cover water damage in older homes?

Sudden damage is usually covered. Damage from long term wear may not be. We document everything to support your claim.
