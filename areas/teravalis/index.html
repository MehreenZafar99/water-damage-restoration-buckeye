Common water damage risks in Teravalis

New plumbing connections

Every new home has hundreds of fittings. Some can loosen as the system goes through its first rounds of pressure changes. Supply lines under sinks, toilet connections and water heater fittings are common spots.

Construction related leaks

A drywall screw or trim nail that grazed a pipe can cause a slow leak inside a wall. Shower pans and tub drains that were not sealed properly can leak into the subfloor. These problems often show up months after move in.

Appliance hookups

Washers, dishwashers and ice makers are often connected during move in. Loose connections are one of the most common early leaks.

First monsoon season

The first monsoon season is often the first real test for a new home's roof, windows, door seals and yard grading. Missed flashing or grading that slopes toward the house can let water in.

Unfinished surroundings

In a growing community, lots around you may still be under construction. Changing grades and open dirt can change how storm water flows during heavy rain.

Your builder warranty

Most new homes come with a builder warranty that covers certain defects for a set period. If you find a leak:

  1. Stop the water at the fixture or main.
  2. Take photos and video before anything is moved.
  3. Write down the date and time.
  4. Notify your builder in writing, following your warranty paperwork.
  5. Start drying. Most warranties and insurance policies expect you to limit further damage.

We provide photos, moisture readings and a written scope you can share with your builder, warranty company or insurance company. Learn more on our new construction water damage page.

Frequently asked questions

My Teravalis home is brand new. Why is it leaking?

New homes can have loose fittings, nicked pipes, unsealed shower pans or appliance hookup issues that show up in the first months or years.

Should I call my builder or you first?

Stop the water, document everything, then notify your builder in writing and call us to start drying. Waiting can make the damage worse.

Will my builder warranty cover the damage?

It depends on the cause and your warranty terms. Our documentation helps either way.

Can new drywall be dried instead of replaced?

Often, yes, if the water was clean and drying starts quickly.

Is my new roof really at risk in monsoon season?

Most new roofs perform well, but missed flashing or loose tiles can show up in the first heavy storms.

Yes, including new neighborhoods as they open.
