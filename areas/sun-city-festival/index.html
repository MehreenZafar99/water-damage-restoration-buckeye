Common water damage risks in Sun City Festival

Leaks while you are away

Many residents travel or spend part of the summer elsewhere. A small leak from a supply line, toilet fill valve or water heater can run for weeks in an empty home. By the time someone returns, water has spread under flooring and mold may have started.

Water heater failures

Buckeye's hard water shortens water heater life. A failing tank in a garage or closet can release a lot of water quickly.

AC condensate overflow

With the AC running constantly through the summer, drain lines can clog and overflow into ceilings and closets. In an empty home, no one hears the AC shut off or sees the stain spreading.

Toilet and bathroom leaks

Worn fill valves, wax rings and supply lines are common sources of bathroom water damage.

Monsoon rain

Heavy storms can push water past patio doors and through roof leaks.

Leaving for the summer? A simple checklist

  • Turn off the water main before you leave, or at least the valves to the washer and water heater.
  • Leave the AC set at a reasonable temperature so the home stays cool and dry.
  • Ask a neighbor or friend to check the home every week or two.
  • Consider a few inexpensive water sensors near the water heater, washer, sinks and AC unit.
  • Have the AC condensate line flushed before you go.

Frequently asked questions

Can you help if I am out of town?

Yes. If a neighbor or family member finds water, they can call us, and we can coordinate with you by phone.

Will I need to move out during drying?

Usually not. Drying equipment is loud but safe. We place it to keep walkways clear. For larger jobs, some people choose to stay elsewhere for a few days.

Can you deal with my insurance company for me?

We document everything and can work directly with your adjuster. You stay in charge of your claim.

What if I found a leak after being away for weeks?

Call right away. Long running leaks often spread further than they look, and mold may have started. We check with moisture meters and thermal cameras.

Do you move furniture?

Yes. Our team moves and protects furniture and belongings as part of the job.

Can a family member be my contact?

Yes. Just let us know who you would like us to update.
