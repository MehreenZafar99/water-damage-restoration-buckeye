Water damage help for Tartesso homes

Tartesso sits out in the western part of Buckeye, north of I-10, with open desert around it and newer homes than many older parts of the city. Many Tartesso homes use PEX plumbing, which is flexible and resists corrosion better than copper. That means fewer slab leaks from pipe wear, but it does not mean no water damage. The problems here just look a little different.

If water is spreading in your home, call (623) 555-0148 right away.

Common water damage risks in Tartesso

Fixture connection failures

PEX lines themselves hold up well, but the fittings and connections at sinks, toilets, water heaters and manifolds can loosen or fail. A single failed fitting can spray water at full pressure.

Irrigation line leaks

Tartesso yards rely on irrigation. Broken or leaking lines near the house can push water toward the foundation, soak the base of stucco walls and create moisture problems at the slab edge.

Appliance leaks

Washer hoses, dishwasher lines and ice maker lines are common failure points in any home, and newer homes are no exception. Move in hookups that were not fully tightened often show up in the first few years.

Water heaters and hard water

Buckeye's hard water shortens water heater life everywhere in the city, including in newer Tartesso homes.

Monsoon storms in open desert

Tartesso's open surroundings mean monsoon wind and dust can hit hard. Wind driven rain can find its way past doors, windows and roof tiles.

What to do first

  1. Shut off the water main or the valve at the leaking fixture.
  2. Turn off power to wet areas if the breaker is safe to reach.
  3. Get water off tile with towels or a wet vacuum made for water, never a household vacuum.
  4. Photograph the damage.
  5. Call (623) 555-0148.

How we help

We extract water, trace hidden moisture with meters and thermal cameras, dry walls, floors and cabinets, and repair the damage. You get a written estimate before work starts and documentation for insurance.

Frequently asked questions

Does PEX plumbing prevent water damage?

It lowers the risk of some problems, like corrosion related leaks, but fittings, fixtures and appliances can still fail.

Can irrigation leaks damage my house?

Yes. Water pooling next to the foundation can soak the base of walls and the slab edge, and it can lead to moisture inside.

My home is fairly new. Could this be a builder issue?

It might be. Document everything and notify your builder in writing. See our new construction water damage page.

Do you serve all of Tartesso?

Yes. We serve every part of Tartesso and the rest of Buckeye.

What should I do while I wait for help?

Stop the water, stay safe around electricity, move valuables and take photos. We will talk you through it on the phone.

Does insurance cover sudden fitting failures?

Sudden leaks are usually covered by homeowners insurance. We document the damage to support your claim.
