Water damage help for Verrado homes

Verrado is Buckeye's best known master planned community. It climbs into the foothills of the White Tank Mountains, with homes built in phases over many years, from the early neighborhoods near Main Street to newer sections higher up the hillside. That mix of home ages and the hillside setting both shape the kind of water damage we see here.

Common water damage risks in Verrado

Slab leaks under tile floors

Many Verrado homes are built on concrete slabs with supply lines running through or under the concrete. In some homes those lines are copper. Over time, copper in contact with concrete and soil can wear, and small leaks form. Because most Verrado homes have large tile areas, a slab leak can spread a long way under the floor before anyone notices a warm spot or damp grout.

Hillside runoff during monsoon storms

Verrado's slopes are part of what makes it beautiful, but they also move water. During heavy monsoon rain, runoff comes down from higher lots and streets. Homes on the downhill side can see water pool against walls, patio doors and garage doors. Stucco walls can hide water that has worked its way in at the base.

Water heaters wearing out from hard water

Buckeye's hard water is tough on water heaters, and the older sections of Verrado are now at the age when many original tanks fail. A garage water heater that splits can send water under the door and into the house.

Irrigation and landscape water

Verrado's landscaping and irrigation systems add another source. Broken irrigation lines or overwatering next to the foundation can push moisture toward the slab and walls.

Two story homes

Many Verrado homes have upstairs bathrooms and laundry rooms. When a leak starts upstairs, it comes through the ceiling of the room below.

What to do first

  1. Turn off the water main. In most Verrado homes it is on the front of the house or in the garage.
  2. Turn off power to wet areas if it is safe to reach the breaker.
  3. Move rugs, furniture and electronics off wet floors.
  4. Take photos of the water and its source.
  5. Call (623) 555-0148

How we help

We find the source, map how far the water spread with moisture meters and thermal cameras, extract standing water and dry the structure. Then we repair drywall, baseboards and flooring so your home looks like it did before. We document everything for your insurance company and give you a written estimate before work begins.

If your HOA needs information about exterior damage or repairs, we can provide photos and documentation you can share.

Frequently asked questions

Are slab leaks common in Verrado?

They are one of the more common causes of water damage we see in Verrado, partly because of how supply lines were run in some homes and partly because of Buckeye's hard water and shifting soil.

My Verrado home is on a slope. Can monsoon water still get in?

Yes. Downhill homes can get runoff from above, and water can pool against walls and doors during heavy storms. Checking grading and drainage before monsoon season helps.

Do you work with Verrado HOA requirements?

We focus on interior water damage, but we can provide documentation and photos if your HOA needs them for exterior repairs.

How fast can drying start?

Drying starts as soon as the water is extracted, often on the first visit. The sooner you call, the more of your home can be saved.

Will insurance cover my Verrado water damage?

Sudden water damage, like a burst pipe or water heater failure, is usually covered. Outside flooding often needs flood insurance. We document the damage for your claim.

Do you serve businesses in Verrado?

Yes. We help shops, offices and restaurants in the Main Street area and across Verrado.
